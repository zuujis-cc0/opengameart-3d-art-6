N64 Lasers
==========

Simple retro 3D models of laser beams imitating those that appear in the video game Star Fox 64, also known as Lylat Wars.

A laser beam in this style is a low-poly teardrop shape with a gradient texture. The texture is just a straight color texture and there is no shading, making the laser beam look bright against dark space backgrounds.

Provided are six .glb files containing 3D models in red, green, blue, yellow, purple and gray colors and their associated gradient textures. Also provided is an Aseprite file that is the source of the textures, a Blender file that is the source of the 3D models and the separate layers of the source Aseprite file for use in making custom colors.

The Aseprite layers are colors in ascending order of layering, from bottom to top, and in brightness/intensity.

Enjoy!
